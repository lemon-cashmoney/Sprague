## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Sprague** is a bold 3D font designed specifically for eye-catching titles and logos, perfect for graphic designers and enthusiasts. This unique display font features a built-in shadow effect that enhances its three-dimensional appearance, making it ideal for retro-themed designs. The **latest version** of Sprague offers 58 glyphs, covering basic Latin characters and numerals, ensuring a playful touch in your projects. 

With the integrated shadow feature, Sprague allows for the creation of vibrant, comic-style text without the need for additional layer effects. While it's perfect for impactful titles, keep in mind that it may not be suitable for lengthy paragraphs or detailed UI text due to its limited character set. Discover the engaging options that Sprague provides for your design needs!
